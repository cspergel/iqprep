Privacy, in plain words.

Effective 17 November 2026. IQ Prep is run by Spergel Health AI LLC.

Playing needs no account

The game runs in your browser. A camper’s name, birthday, answers, scores and settings are saved on your device only, in the browser’s own storage. We do not receive them. You can export a backup file, or erase a camper’s progress, from the Grown-ups area.

Children

IQ Prep is made for children to play, so it is built not to collect personal information from them. There are no accounts, and nothing a child types in the game is sent to us.

Cookies

IQ Prep itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The ads on the parents’ pages come from Google, which may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure them; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

The parents’ pages of this website (this one, the home page and the page-not-found page) show ads from Google AdSense. We ask Google for non-personalised ads, which are not based on a visitor’s past browsing. There are no ads inside the game at /play/, so children playing never see them. Visitors in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent through Google’s message.

Who else handles data

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the pages and the game. We do not collect, sell or share personal information ourselves.

Questions

Write to Craig@spergelhealth.ai. If we change this policy, the new version and its date will appear here.
