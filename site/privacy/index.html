Privacy, in plain words.

Effective 17 November 2026. Updated 17 November 2026 to add password reset emails. IQ Prep is run by Spergel Health AI LLC.

Playing needs no account

The game runs in your browser. A camper’s name, birthday, answers, scores and settings are saved on your device only, in the browser’s own storage. We do not receive them. You can export a backup file, or erase a camper’s progress, from the Grown-ups area.

Parent accounts are for adults only

An account is optional and lets a parent or guardian back up progress and restore it on another device. To create one we ask for an email address, a password and your birth month. The birth month is used once to confirm you are an adult and is not stored. If the answer shows someone is under 18, account sign-up closes on that browser.

For an account we keep:

  • your email address;
  • a salted hash of your password (never the password itself);
  • when you confirmed you are an adult and agreed to this policy;
  • your sign-in sessions;
  • your latest backup, if you make one.

Backups are trimmed on your device before they are sent: children’s names are replaced with “Camper 1”, “Camper 2” and so on, birthdays are cut to the month (which the age-based scoring needs), and the grown-up PIN is left out. What remains is game progress: answers, practice estimates, streaks and patches.

We email you only when you ask to reset your password. The email is sent through Cloudflare and contains a one-time link that expires after an hour.

You can delete the account and its backup at any time from Grown-ups, then Settings, then Parent account. Deletion is immediate.

Children

IQ Prep is made for children to play, so it is built not to collect personal information from them. Children cannot create accounts, and nothing a child types in the game is sent to us.

Cookies

We set two cookies, both only when they are needed: a sign-in cookie for parent accounts, and a cookie that keeps account sign-up closed after an under-18 answer. There are no analytics or tracking cookies.

Advertising

There are no ads today. If we add them, they will appear only on the parents’ pages of this website, never inside the game, and they will be non-personalised ads from Google AdSense, set up for a site used by children. We will update this page before that happens.

Who else handles data

The site and parent accounts are hosted by Cloudflare, which processes requests and stores account data for us. We do not sell or share personal information.

Questions

Write to Craig@spergelhealth.ai. If we change this policy, the new version and its date will appear here.
